About Heidelberg - SORDZ(S-Line)

The Baumann NUP 650 FO is a high-performance folder-gluer machine designed for the packaging and printing industry. This machine is specifically engineered for folding and gluing corrugated cardboard boxes, offering exceptional precision, speed, and reliability. Key features of the NUP 650 FO include its advanced servo-driven technology, high-speed folding capabilities, and precise gluing system. The machine is ideal for medium to large-sized packaging converters, providing numerous benefits such as increased productivity, reduced waste, and improved product quality. Typical applications of the Baumann NUP 650 FO include folding and gluing of corrugated boxes, displays, and other paperboard products.

Key Technical Aspects:

Dimensions and Specifications The Baumann NUP 650 FO has a machine length of 13,120 mm, a machine width of 2,400 mm, and a machine height of 2,200 mm. The machine weighs approximately 12,000 kg and has a maximum power consumption of 24 kW. The NUP 650 FO has a maximum sheet size capacity of 1,500 mm x 650 mm and a minimum sheet size capacity of 300 mm x 200 mm.

Paper Handling he Baumann NUP 650 FO features a high-capacity feeder with a maximum pile height of 1,200 mm, allowing for efficient and continuous production. The machine is equipped with a vacuum-assisted sheet transport system, ensuring precise and gentle handling of the paperboard sheets. The NUP 650 FO has a maximum output capacity of 18,000 sheets per hour.

Printing Unit: The Baumann NUP 650 FO does not have a built-in printing unit, as it is designed specifically for folding and gluing applications. However, it can be easily integrated with various printing machines, such as flexographic or offset printers, to create a comprehensive packaging production line.

Ink System: As the Baumann NUP 650 FO is not a printing machine, it does not have an ink system.

Plate Cylinder: The Baumann NUP 650 FO does not have a plate cylinder, as it is not a printing machine. Instead, it features a sophisticated folding and gluing system, which includes a series of precision-engineered folding rollers and a high-precision gluing unit.
