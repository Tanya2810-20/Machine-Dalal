The Duplo System 3500 is an automated booklet making and finishing system used in print production environments — especially suited for commercial printers, digital printers, copy shops, and in-plant print departments.

Core Functionality

This machine integrates multiple finishing steps into one streamlined workflow:

  • Automated Sheet Feeding: Sheets fed from output (collated or uncollated) of digital or offset printers.

  • Staple Binding & Folding: Collated sheets are stitched (saddle-stitched) and folded into booklets.
